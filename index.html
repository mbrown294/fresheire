FRESH EIRE

Bringing the Irish magic to London

We don't miss Ireland. Sure we're grand over here.

We miss the bits. The Pints. The fifteens. The spice bags. The Ballymaloe on everything. A Football Special (IYKYK) Tayto, beyond cheese and onion. The craic and the nonsense and the thing where someone brings out a traybake and suddenly it's a whole thing.

So we're bringing the Irish magic to London. Fresh Eire is just getting started.

Step One

A market stall. Obviously.

We've got ambitions for all sorts. But every great Irish enterprise starts somewhere.

Ours starts with a table, some homemade bakes, and the firm belief that if we build it, they will come. A proper Irish food and drink stall at a London market. Coffee, craic, and more fifteens than is medically advisable.

☕
