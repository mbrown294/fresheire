About

Ana Brown

I have spent over 15 years working alongside senior lawyers at international, US and offshore law firms. At Conyers, I led business development across the firm's global offices. At Maples Group, I drove BD and marketing for the largest legal funds team in Cayman. At Ropes & Gray in London, I supported a broad range of practice groups at one of the world's leading law firms.

I studied Jurisprudence at the University of Oxford before completing an MSc at the London School of Economics. I have particular experience with the Cayman, Bermuda, BVI, US, UK and Asian markets, working with corporate and litigation teams to expand their client reach.

I started A to B Consulting because too many firms either lack dedicated BD support or struggle to get value from generic approaches. My work is tailored, practical and focused on outcomes.

Approach

How we work together

No bloated scopes. Every engagement follows a clear structure that keeps you informed and in control.

1

Discovery

A 30-minute conversation to understand your firm's goals, current BD activity, and where the gaps are. No commitment, no pitch.

2

Proposal

A clear scope of work with defined deliverables, timeline and fees. You know exactly what you are getting before anything starts.

3

Execution

Monthly priorities, regular check-ins and transparent reporting. Activity is documented, outcomes are measured, and next steps are agreed together.

Engagement Models

Flexible support, no long-term lock-in

Not every firm needs a full-time BD hire. Choose the model that fits where you are right now.

Project

A defined scope with clear deliverables and timeline. Well suited to directory submissions, market analysis or event management.

Fractional Retainer

Ongoing monthly support with agreed priorities and regular reviews. Senior BD expertise without the cost of a full-time hire.

Interim

Filling a gap in your BD function while you recruit or restructure. Full integration with your team on a temporary basis.

Why A to B

Built for the offshore legal market

The Cayman Islands is a unique market. Small community, global reach, fierce competition for directory rankings, and business development that depends on relationships built over years, not months.

We have worked in this market for over seven years and understand its dynamics from the inside.

  • Directory relationships Established relationships with editors and researchers at Chambers, Legal 500 and IFLR.
  • Senior-level experience 15 years working with managing partners, practice heads and fee earners at leading international firms.
  • Right-sized for your firm Senior BD capability without the overhead of a full-time hire or the inefficiency of a generalist agency.
