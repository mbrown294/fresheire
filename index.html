Fresh Eire

Irish food, drink & nonsense in London

We're two friends from Ireland living in London. We love it here, but we miss the bits. The fifteens. The spice bags. The Ballymaloe on everything. A cold Football Special on a Saturday. So we're doing something about it.

↓

Step One: A Market Stall

We've got big plans, but we're starting where it matters: a stall at Bermondsey Blue Market, bringing the best of Irish food, drink and craic to South London every Saturday.

Think imports you can't find anywhere else, homemade bakes your granny would approve of, proper coffee, and craft Irish beers you've never heard of.

  • Tayto
  • Ballymaloe Relish
  • Football Special
  • Barry's Tea
  • Irish Dairy Milk
  • Manhattan Popcorn
  • Fifteens
  • Soda Bread
  • Potato Bread
  • Traybakes
  • Craft Irish Beer

This is just the start. We have ambitions for much more than a stall, but sure look, you have to start somewhere. Expect more from us.
